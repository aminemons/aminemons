I'm a 4th-year **Micro & Nanoelectronics** student at ENSNN, Algeria.

I'm also interested in applying **AI to engineering problems**, particularly semiconductor devices, physical modeling, and optimization. I still enjoy building software along the way.

- **Microhotplate Optimization** — Simulation and AI-assisted optimization of microheaters for gas sensors.
- **Zynq Battery Monitoring** — FPGA-based battery monitoring with state estimation and neural-network acceleration.
